Research

Structure, guided by experiment.

  1. Integrative complex modeling

    Ion-mobility mass spectrometry measures the size and shape of a protein complex, but not its atomic structure. I use those collision cross sections to guide Rosetta docking of AlphaFold subunits. The resulting IM-Complex score improved challenging targets relative to AlphaFold-Multimer. Published in Analytical Chemistry, 2026.

  2. Cryo-EM and ion mobility together

    With Jacob B. Howard, I worked on CRIM, a Rosetta score that combines low-resolution cryo-EM density with ion-mobility data for monomer structure prediction. Co-first author paper in Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation, 2026. Current work is an experimentally guided diffusion model.

  3. Energetics, docking, and design

    Deep learning models can track umbrella-sampling energetics, tested on troponin C with ProteinMPNN and ThermoMPNN during a visit to the Meiler Lab (Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, 2026). Related projects include small-molecule docking for glioblastoma targets and an AI pipeline for antibacterial MurA binders.
